Privacy Policy for FXBlockFall

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FXBlockFall handles information when you use the application.

2. Information Collection and Use

FXBlockFall is a simple Tetris-style game.

The game may allow a player to enter a username and saves game-related information, such as high scores and game state. This information is used to identify the player's game profile and preserve game progress.

FXBlockFall does not intentionally collect personal information that identifies users beyond the information described in this policy.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

FXBlockFall saves high scores and game state. This information is used to maintain the player's progress and game records.

The game does not share this information with third parties, according to the developer's current description.

4. Third-Party Services

FXBlockFall does not use third-party services, analytics, advertising services, or external APIs.

5. Children's Privacy

FXBlockFall does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been collected through the game, please contact the developer.
