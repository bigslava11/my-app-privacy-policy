Privacy Policy for Pixel Serpent

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Introduction

BigSlava respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Pixel Serpent game handles user information.

Information Collection

Pixel Serpent does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to BigSlava, provided the game is used as distributed and no additional data-collecting services are added.

Data Storage

The game runs locally on your Windows device. Any game data saved locally remains on your device and is not transmitted to BigSlava.

Third-Party Services

Pixel Serpent does not use third-party analytics or advertising services, provided none are included in the distributed application.

Children's Privacy

Pixel Serpent does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact BigSlava through the developer's support page.
